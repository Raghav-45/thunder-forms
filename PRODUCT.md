# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Creators and teams who need to build forms, quizzes, and data-collection workflows.

## Product Purpose

Thunder Forms is an AI-native form builder that lets people create and customize forms, collect responses, grade quizzes, and turn submissions into usable insights.

## Positioning

Thunder Forms combines drag-and-drop form building with AI-assisted generation, response analytics, quiz grading, and Google Sheets synchronization.

## Operating Context

Users create and manage forms from a dashboard, share public form links, review responses and analytics, and connect Google services for imports, uploads, and Sheets synchronization.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Forms can be built with drag and drop or generated with AI.
- The product supports response collection, quiz grading, analytics, Google Sheets synchronization, Google Forms import, and Drive uploads.
- AI generation requires a Gemini API key; some integrations and analytics require separately configured services.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Thunder Forms.
- Product promise: “the form builder that thinks for you.”

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing web product and user-facing copy in this repository.
- Production site: https://thunderforms.in/.
- No customer testimonials, benchmarks, pricing, or other proof claims have been confirmed for future design work.

## Product Principles

- Make form creation faster without sacrificing control.
- Turn submissions into clear, actionable information.
- Keep integrations close to the form workflow.
- Support both simple collection and assessed quiz workflows.
